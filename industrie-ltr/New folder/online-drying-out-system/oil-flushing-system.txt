## Oil Flushing System

The Oil Flushing System is designed for cleaning and flushing transformer oil circuits and associated piping systems to remove sludge, dirt particles, and other contaminants. During transformer manufacturing, installation, or maintenance, oil systems may accumulate impurities that can affect insulation performance and equipment reliability. Proper flushing ensures that the system operates with clean oil and improves overall transformer performance. 

Spera Oil Flushing Systems circulate transformer oil at controlled flow rates through high-efficiency filtration units to remove contaminants and restore oil cleanliness. The system provides reliable flushing performance and ensures contamination-free oil circuits before transformer commissioning or servicing.

### Questions? You’re covered

**01. What is an Oil Flushing System used for?**
An Oil Flushing System is used to clean transformer oil circuits and remove contaminants such as sludge, dirt, and debris before commissioning or maintenance operations.

**02. Why is oil flushing required?**
Oil flushing removes impurities that accumulate during installation or maintenance, helping ensure reliable operation and preventing premature equipment failure. 

**03. How does an Oil Flushing System work?**
The system circulates oil through pipelines and transformer components while passing it through filtration units to remove contaminants and improve oil cleanliness.

**04. When should oil flushing be performed?**
Oil flushing is typically performed before commissioning, after major repairs, or during transformer maintenance to ensure clean oil systems and reliable operation.
