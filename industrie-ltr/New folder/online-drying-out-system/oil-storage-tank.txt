
## Oil Storage Tank

The Oil Storage Tank is designed for safe and contamination-free storage of transformer insulating oil used in transformer manufacturing and maintenance. Proper storage of transformer oil is essential to preserve its insulating and cooling properties and maintain its performance before use in electrical equipment. 

Spera Oil Storage Tanks are manufactured with robust construction and reliable sealing arrangements to store clean or used transformer oil safely for long periods. The tanks are designed for easy oil transfer and integration with oil filtration and handling systems, ensuring reliable and efficient operation.

### Questions? You’re covered

**01. What is an Oil Storage Tank used for?**
An Oil Storage Tank is used to safely store transformer insulating oil so that it can be used when required for transformer filling or maintenance operations. 

**02. Why is proper oil storage important?**
Proper oil storage prevents contamination and degradation of transformer oil, helping maintain its insulating and cooling properties. 

**03. What capacities are available for Oil Storage Tanks?**
Oil Storage Tanks are available in a wide range of capacities, typically from about 1,000 liters to 90,000 liters depending on application requirements. 

**04. Can the Oil Storage Tank be connected to filtration systems?**
Yes, Oil Storage Tanks can be integrated with oil filtration and handling systems for storage and transfer of clean or used transformer oil.
