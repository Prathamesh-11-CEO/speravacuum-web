## Oil Transfer Pump

The Oil Transfer Pump is designed for efficient and safe transfer of transformer insulating oil between storage tanks, filtration units, and transformers during manufacturing and maintenance operations. Proper oil transfer is essential to prevent contamination and ensure reliable performance of transformers and electrical equipment. 

Spera Oil Transfer Pumps provide smooth and controlled oil movement with reliable pumping performance and easy operation. The system enables quick filling, draining, and circulation of transformer oil between tanks and transformers, reducing downtime and ensuring safe oil handling.

### Questions? You’re covered

**01. What is an Oil Transfer Pump used for?**
An Oil Transfer Pump is used to move transformer oil between storage tanks, drums, filtration units, and transformers during filling, draining, or maintenance operations. 

**02. Can the Oil Transfer Pump be used with filtration systems?**
Yes, Oil Transfer Pumps are commonly connected with oil filtration and handling systems for controlled oil movement between equipment. 

**03. Is the Oil Transfer Pump portable?**
Yes, many Oil Transfer Pumps are designed as portable or trolley-mounted units for easy movement and on-site transformer maintenance. 

**04. Why is a dedicated Oil Transfer Pump required?**
A dedicated Oil Transfer Pump ensures safe, contamination-free oil transfer and reduces the time required for transformer oil filling and draining operations.
